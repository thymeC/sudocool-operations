**Episode 27 · BUG+1** (`bug_plus_one`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **BUG+1**: near the end, every empty cell is bivalue except **one** trivalue cell — the extra candidate is the answer.

**BUG** = Bivalue Universal Grave: if every empty were bivalue with paired logic everywhere, the grid would have two solutions — dead.  
One cell still has three candidates — to avoid BUG, that cell must take the **extra** digit; the other two would complete the deadly bivalue grid.

On the board: all empties bivalue except r5c5 {3,6,7} — place **7**.

Use late game when the grid is almost all bivalue — not every puzzle.

Practice: confirm BUG+1 shape, place the extra digit, watch the rest collapse.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: All bivalue but one cell with three — the odd candidate is the placement.**
