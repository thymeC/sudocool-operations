**Episode 16 · Two-String Kite** (`two_string_kite`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Two-String Kite**: a digit has only two cells in one row and two in one column; the two “free ends” land in the **same box** — the kite is tied. A cell seeing both ends cannot hold the digit.

Structure: row has two 9s, column has two 9s; one endpoint is the row/column cross; the other two free ends sit in one box — the two knots.  
One knot must be 9, so the two free ends can’t both be false — a cell seeing both loses 9.

On the board: green row and column ends; r5c1 sees both — delete 9.

**How to find:** pick a digit; find row-with-two + column-with-two; check if free ends share a box.  
Sibling of Skyscraper: parallel lines vs one row + one column.

Practice twice — tie the kite, eliminate.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: One row and one column, two cells each, free ends same box — a cell seeing both ends loses the digit.**
