**Episode 25 · Multi-Coloring** (`multi_coloring`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Multi-Coloring**: when one chain isn’t enough, color **several** strong-link chains for the same digit and let colors talk across chains.

Same-digit strong links color green/yellow (or more pairs).  
Separate chains may connect through **weak links** (seeing each other): opposite colors link chains — exclusions propagate.  
Short form: a cell seeing conflicting colors loses the digit; one color contradicted by another → wipe that color.

On the board: digit **6** with green/yellow and blue/orange chains; green sees blue — r4c7 sees yellow and blue — delete 6.

Color every strong chain first, then scan between chains.

Practice cross-chain logic.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Many chains, many colors — colors that see each other propagate; see opposing colors, delete the digit.**
