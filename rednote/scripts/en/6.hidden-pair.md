**Episode 6 · Hidden Pair** (`hidden_pair`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Intermediate technique — **Hidden Pair**: two digits in a house can only appear in the same two cells. Strip every other candidate from those two cells.

Opposite of a Naked Pair:  
Naked Pair — look **from the cells** — two cells already show only {A, B}.  
Hidden Pair — look **from the digits** — A and B only appear in two cells, even if those cells still hold extra notes.

Row 3: candidates 4 and 7 appear only in r3c2 and r3c6.  
No other empty in the row can be 4 or 7.  
Those two cells must be 4 and 7 — eliminate the extras (1 and 9).

Before the elimination, r3c2 was {1,4,7,9} and r3c6 was {4,7,9} — not a Naked Pair.  
You found it by asking “where can 4 and 7 go in this row?” After trimming, it may look like a Naked Pair.

**Rules of thumb:**  
Two cells showing exactly {A,B} → Naked Pair → eliminate A and B elsewhere.  
A and B only in two cells but those cells have more notes → Hidden Pair → trim those two cells.  
For Hidden Pairs, **track digit positions**, not just cell note counts.

Practice two puzzles — find the Hidden Pair, then tell naked from hidden.

Homework or five in a row in the app.

**Mnemonic: Two digits, two cells only — delete the clutter inside those cells.**  
For Hidden Pairs, watch where digits land.
