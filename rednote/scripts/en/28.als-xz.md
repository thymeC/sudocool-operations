**Episode 28 · ALS-XZ** (`als_xz`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **ALS-XZ**: two **Almost Locked Sets** bridged by shared digit **X** eliminate target digit **Z**.

**ALS:** n cells, n+1 distinct candidates — one digit short of locked.  
Some digit X must appear in the set.  
Two ALSs each contain Z, share restricted X — if Z were nowhere in either set, contradictions follow — Z must appear in one ALS.  
Cells seeing all Z placements in both sets drop Z.

On the board: ALS A (three cells) and ALS B (two cells) share X=2, target Z=5; r4c6 sees both — delete 5.

Entry point to ALS: cells count vs candidate count minus one; roles X and Z.

Practice twice.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Two almost-locked groups, X bridges — a cell seeing both groups’ Z loses Z.**
