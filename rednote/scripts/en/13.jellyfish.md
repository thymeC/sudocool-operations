**Episode 13 · Jellyfish** (`jellyfish`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Jellyfish**, the **4×4 fish**: four rows and four columns. A digit’s placements in four rows all lie within the same four columns — eliminate that digit from the other cells in those columns.

Recap: X-Wing 2×2, Swordfish 3×3, Jellyfish 4×4.

On the board: digit **2** spans four rows and four columns — like a jellyfish.  
Delete 2 outside the fish body in those four columns.

Jellyfish is rare in practice, but the search is identical: map placements, check column overlap. Often reveals a smaller fish after eliminations.

Practice both orientations — don’t mix rows and columns.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Four rows, four columns — delete the digit from the rest of those columns.**  
2-, 3-, and 4-fish — one logic, different size.
