**Episode 24 · Simple Coloring** (`simple_coloring`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Simple Coloring**: one digit, alternate two colors along strong links — color does the eliminating.

Strong link: a digit has only two cells in a row, column, or box — one must be true.  
Start green on any cell; alternate yellow along links.  
Same color is all-true or all-false.  
Rule 1: two same-color cells see each other → that color is false — remove all candidates of that color.  
Rule 2: a cell sees both colors → that digit cannot go there.

On the board: digit **9** colored green/yellow; two green cells share row 4 — green false; r7c5 sees both colors — delete 9.

Pick digits with few placements; paint as you find strong links.

Practice both rules.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: One digit, two colors on strong links — same color peers kill the color; one cell sees both colors, drop the digit.**
