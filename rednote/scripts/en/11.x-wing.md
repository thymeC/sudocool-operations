**Episode 11 · X-Wing** (`x_wing`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Welcome to **advanced** techniques — **X-Wing**, also called a **2×2 fish**: a digit appears in exactly two cells in each of two rows, and those four cells share the same two columns — a rectangle. Then eliminate that digit from all other cells in those two columns.

On the board: digit **1** has only two green cells in row 5 and two in row 8; the four corners align — X-Wing.  
Each row’s 1 is either in the left or right column of the pattern; those columns’ 1s are consumed by the corners.  
So everywhere else in those two columns, delete 1.

**How to find:** pick a digit; scan rows for “only two cells left”; if two rows’ footprints share the **same two columns**, you have an X-Wing. Columns-first works too.

Practice: confirm the rectangle, clean both columns.  
Try the column-based version — don’t flip row and column.

Homework or five in the app.

**Mnemonic: Two rows, two cells each, columns aligned — delete the digit from the rest of those columns.**  
When a digit’s only row placements share two columns, that’s X-Wing — the foundation of all fish patterns.
