**Episode 9 · Naked Triple** (`naked_triple`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Intermediate — **Naked Triple**: scale up the Naked Pair — three cells whose candidates together use exactly three digits. Those digits are locked to those three cells; quads work the same way.

Same logic as a pair, but **three cells, three digits** (or four and four).  
Each cell doesn’t need all three notes — the **union** of their candidates must be exactly those digits.

Row 2: three green cells together only contain 1, 2, and 3 — so 1, 2, and 3 must occupy those three cells.  
Remove 1, 2, and 3 from the other cells in the row.

A Naked Quad is four digits in four cells.

Practice: find a Naked Triple and clean the house.  
Confirm the union is exactly three digits — no fourth sneaking in.

More drills in the Sudoku Dojo app — five correct in a row.

**Mnemonic: N cells, union of exactly N digits — elsewhere in the house, eliminate those N digits.**
