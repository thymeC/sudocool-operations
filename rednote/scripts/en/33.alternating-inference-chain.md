**Episode 33 · Alternating Inference Chain (AIC)** (`aic`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Alternating Inference Chain (AIC)**: every chain technique in one rule — **strong** and **weak** links alternate; endpoint candidates — at least one is true.

**Strong link:** digit only two places in a unit, or two candidates in one bivalue cell — one must be true.  
**Weak link:** two cells that cannot both be true — at least one false.  
Alternate strong, weak, strong, weak…  
Endpoints: if a cell sees both and would host the linked digits, eliminate accordingly.

On the board: AIC from r2c5’s 4 to r7c8’s 4 — other 4s on the line between drop out.

Skyscraper, kite, XY-Chain, coloring — AIC special cases. Master strong vs weak and the series clicks together.

Practice build an AIC; translate a Skyscraper into AIC notation.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Strong and weak alternate — endpoints force truth; see both ends, eliminate the digit. AIC ties every chain together.**
