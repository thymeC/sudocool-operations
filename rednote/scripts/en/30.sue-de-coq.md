**Episode 30 · Sue de Coq** (`sue_de_coq`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Sue de Coq**: at a **box–line intersection**, a tiny set of cells has candidates that **exactly** fit a locked pattern — digits promised inside the cluster are eliminated outside on the line and inside the box.

Standard shape: cells on the line plus cells in the box at the crossing; candidate counts match the “n cells, n+k digits split” rule.  
Some digits must land inside the intersection block → delete those digits from other cells on the line and in the box per the pattern.

On the board: intersection cells {2,7} and {2,7,9}; apply line vs box eliminations for 2, 7, and shared digits.

Hardest to spot, but always at box–line crosses — check if candidates are “tight.”

Practice: identify block, confirm counts, eliminate outside.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Box meets line — candidates fit exactly; inside the block digits stay, outside they go.**
