**Episode 0 · Sudoku Basics** — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Welcome to Sudoku Dojo — in this series we’ll walk from beginner moves to advanced logic, one technique at a time.

**Visual:** App logo; 9×9 grid with row, column, and box highlights.

The rules in thirty seconds: fill every row, column, and 3×3 box with digits 1 through 9 — no repeats.  
You never need to guess. Every cell we fill comes from logic you can explain.

What you’ll use on the board: **candidates** — small notes showing what might still fit in a cell.  
We’ll teach you when to fill a number outright, when to trim notes, and when a pattern locks digits in place.

This course has three tiers: **beginner**, **intermediate**, and **advanced**.  
Stick with the order — each episode builds on the last.

Next up: **Full House** — when a house has only one empty cell left, the answer is already decided.

Practice anytime in the app. Let’s go.
