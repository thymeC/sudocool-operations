**Episode 15 · Skyscraper** (`skyscraper`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Skyscraper**: on two parallel lines, a digit has only two cells each. One end aligns on a shared column (the base); the other ends offset — those offset ends are the **roofs**. Any cell that sees both roofs cannot hold the digit.

Structure: digit **d** has two cells in row A and two in row B; one cell per row shares a column (aligned bases); the other two cells are the roofs.  
The shared column means **d** is in one roof or the other — at least one roof is true.  
Any cell seeing both roofs eliminates **d**.

On the board: **6** in rows 2 and 6, bases aligned in column 7.  
Roofs at r2c2 and r6c5.  
Cell r6c2 sees both roofs — remove 6.

Skyscraper is a single-digit strong link: aligned base = either/or; roofs force an elimination. Works with rows or columns as the “building sides.”

Practice: find base and roofs, eliminate common peers.  
Try column-based skyscrapers.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Two lines, two cells each, bases aligned, roofs skew — a cell seeing both roofs loses the digit.**  
Skyscrapers are among the easiest chain patterns to spot.
