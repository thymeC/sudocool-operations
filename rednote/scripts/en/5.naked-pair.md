**Episode 5 · Naked Pair** (`naked_pair`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. We’re moving to **intermediate** — **Naked Pair**: in one row, column, or box, two cells share exactly the same two candidates. Those two digits are locked to those two cells.

Unlike a Naked Single — one cell, one candidate —  
a Naked Pair is **two cells, same two digits**.  
They must consume those digits, so every **other** cell in that house loses those two candidates.

On this board, row 5: two green cells each show only {1, 2}.  
So 1 and 2 for row 5 live in those two cells.  
Remove 1 and 2 from every other cell in the row.

**How to spot it:** scan a house for two cells whose notes are identical and only two digits — then eliminate those digits from the rest of the house.

Practice two puzzles.  
First: tap hint, find the pair, clean the house.  
Second: try another row or box — make sure you’re **reading cells**, not guessing.

Homework — or five straight in the app.

**Mnemonic: Two cells, same pair — elsewhere in the house, drop both digits.**
