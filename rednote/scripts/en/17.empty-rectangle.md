**Episode 17 · Empty Rectangle** (`empty_rectangle`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Empty Rectangle**: in a box, every placement for a digit lies on one row and one column — an L-shaped cross; the rest of the box is empty. Pair with a strong link on another line to eliminate at the intersection.

In the center-left box, all **4** candidates sit on one row and one column — empty rectangle; the corner is the elbow.  
Elsewhere, **4** has only two cells on a line — strong link: 4 is at the elbow or the other end.  
Either way, a cell at the intersection of that line and the cross cannot be 4.

On the board: cross inside the box; row 6 has two 4s.  
Intersection r6c4 loses 4.

**How to find:** scan boxes for L-shaped digit footprints; check matching lines for two-cell strong links.

Practice both elbow orientations.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Box forms a cross for one digit, line gives a strong pair — eliminate at the extended intersection.**
