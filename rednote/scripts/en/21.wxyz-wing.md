**Episode 21 · WXYZ-Wing** (`wxyz_wing`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **WXYZ-Wing**: four cells, four distinct candidates W, X, Y, Z; three wing cells each contain **Z**. A cell seeing all three wings cannot be **Z**.

Pivot holds three candidates (e.g. W, X, Y); three bivalue wings each contain Z plus one of W, X, Y.  
If pivot takes Z, done; if pivot takes W, X, or Y, the matching wing forces Z — Z always lands on a wing.  
Cells seeing all three wings drop Z.

On the board: pivot r4c5 {1,5,7}; wings {1,9}, {5,9}, {7,9}; target r6c4 loses 9.

**How to find:** anchor digit Z, gather Z bivalue wings; three wings whose other digits match a three-candidate pivot.  
XY-, XYZ-Wings are smaller cases.

Practice; compare to XYZ-Wing.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Four cells, four digits, three wings share Z — a cell seeing all three wings loses Z.**
