**Episode 23 · XY-Chain** (`xy_chain`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **XY-Chain**: link bivalue cells so neighbors share a digit. Endpoints keep digits **a** and **b**; at least one endpoint digit is true — a cell seeing both endpoints loses **a** and **b**.

Walk the chain: each cell is either/or; shared digits propagate.  
Endpoints a and b cannot both be false — a cell seeing both endpoints eliminates a and b.

On the board: five bivalue cells; endpoints 4 and 8; r5c5 sees both — delete 4 and 8.

XY-Chain generalizes XY-Wing (three cells); Remote Pairs is the all-same-pair special case.

Practice; rewrite a short XY-Wing as a chain.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Bivalue chain — endpoints each keep one digit; a cell seeing both endpoints loses both.**
