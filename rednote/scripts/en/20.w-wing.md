**Episode 20 · W-Wing** (`w_wing`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **W-Wing**: two bivalue cells with the **same** pair {X,Y}, linked by a strong link on **X**. Any cell seeing both endpoints cannot be **Y**.

Both endpoints are “X or Y.”  
The strong link says X must be at one endpoint — so the other endpoint must be Y — at least one endpoint is Y.  
Cells seeing both endpoints eliminate Y.

On the board: r2c3 and r8c6 both {4,8}; column 2 has 4 only in r2c3 and r8c3 — strong link on 4.  
r8c4 sees both endpoints — delete 8.

**How to find:** two matching bivalue cells + a line where digit X (or Y) has exactly those two endpoints.

Practice; try the link on the other digit.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Two cells both XY, strong link on one digit — a cell seeing both loses the other digit.**  
W-Wing = bivalue pair + single-digit strong link.
