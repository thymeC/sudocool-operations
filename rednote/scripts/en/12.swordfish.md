**Episode 12 · Swordfish** (`swordfish`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Swordfish**, the **3×3 fish**: X-Wing scaled to three rows and three columns. A digit’s placements in three rows all fall within the same three columns — delete that digit from the other cells in those columns.

Works with rows and columns swapped: three columns’ placements confined to three rows → eliminate from those rows.

On the board: digit **4** forms a three-by-three fish.  
Remove 4 from every cell in those columns outside the three defining rows.

Note: a row doesn’t need exactly three placements — only that none fall outside the three columns. A column with zero placements in those rows can still work.

Practice: list three row footprints, confirm columns, eliminate.  
Flip to three columns → three rows.

Homework or app — five in a row.

**Mnemonic: Three rows, same three columns — delete the digit from the rest of those columns.**  
X-Wing is 2-fish; Swordfish is 3-fish — same recipe, bigger net.
