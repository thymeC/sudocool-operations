**Episode 14 · Finned Fish** (`finned_fish`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Finned Fish**: almost a standard fish — one extra placement sticks out like a fin on the fish’s back. Under the right box condition, the fin still enables eliminations.

Review: a fish keeps each row’s placements inside the same column set.  
A **fin** is an extra cell in one row that breaks outside that column set.  
The fin can’t join the fish logic — but if the fin shares a box with a fish **corner**, you can eliminate: cells in that box seen by both fin and corner lose the digit.

On the board: digit **8** in three rows mostly fits two columns, but row 3 has an extra placement — the fin.  
Fin and top-left corner share the top-left box.  
Cells in that box seen by both fin and corner lose 8.

Key: fin and a fish corner must share a box, or the fin doesn’t help.

Practice: identify fish and fin, same box as corner, eliminate.  
Try a fin without a shared box — see why nothing deletes.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Fish minus one cell — that’s the fin; fin and corner same box — eliminate inside the box.**  
Finned fish rescue near-miss fish patterns.
