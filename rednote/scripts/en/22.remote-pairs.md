**Episode 22 · Remote Pairs** (`remote_pairs`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Remote Pairs**: a chain of bivalue cells with the **same** pair {X,Y}. Cells an **odd** number of steps apart must hold opposite digits — a cell seeing both eliminates **X** and **Y**.

Several cells all {3,7}; neighbors in the chain force one 3 and one 7.  
Count steps from the start: endpoints differing by an **odd** step count must be 3 and 7 respectively.  
A cell seeing both endpoints loses both digits.

On the board: five {3,7} cells chained; r1c2 and r6c8 are odd steps apart; r6c1 sees both — delete 3 and 7.

Requirements: entire chain same pair; elimination cell must see **both** odd/even-mismatched endpoints.

Practice; try two even-step cells — no elimination.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Same pair chained — odd/even endpoints opposite; a cell seeing both loses both digits.**
