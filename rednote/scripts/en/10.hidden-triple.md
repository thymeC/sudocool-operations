**Episode 10 · Hidden Triple** (`hidden_triple`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Intermediate — **Hidden Triple**: three digits in a house appear only in three cells. Remove every other candidate from those three cells; Hidden Quads follow the same idea.

Versus Naked Triple:  
Naked — from the cells — three cells’ notes union to three digits.  
Hidden — from the digits — three digits’ positions union to three cells, often with extra notes still inside.

Column 7: digits 5, 6, and 8 only appear in three green cells.  
Those three digits must live in those three cells.  
Delete all other candidates from those cells.

**How to find:** list positions of A, B, C in the house; if the union is exactly three cells, it’s a Hidden Triple — even if cells still show other candidates.

Practice: map three digits’ positions, confirm three cells, trim extras.  
After trimming, check whether it became a Naked Triple.

App practice — five in a row.

**Mnemonic: N digits in only N cells — delete the extras inside those cells.**
