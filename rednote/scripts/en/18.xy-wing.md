**Episode 18 · XY-Wing** (`xy_wing`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **XY-Wing**: three bivalue cells — pivot **XY**, wings **XZ** and **YZ**. Any cell that sees **both** wings cannot be **Z**.

Logic: pivot is X or Y.  
If pivot is X, the XZ wing forces Z; if pivot is Y, the YZ wing forces Z.  
Either way, Z appears on a wing — cells seeing both wings drop Z.

On the board: pivot r3c1 {2,7}, left wing r1c4 {2,9}, right wing r8c1 {7,9}.  
Cells r1c1 and r8c4 see both wings — delete 9.

**How to find:** scan bivalue cells for pivot + two wings; wings need not see each other, but eliminations must see both wings.

Practice twice.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Pivot XY, wings share Z — a cell seeing both wings loses Z.**  
XY-Wing is the foundation of all wing techniques.
