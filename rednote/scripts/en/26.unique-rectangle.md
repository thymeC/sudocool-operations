**Episode 26 · Unique Rectangle** (`unique_rectangle`) — Full voice-over script

No guessing in Sudoku. Advanced — **Unique Rectangle**: the technique that uses “Sudoku must have **one** solution.” Four cells form a rectangle across **two** boxes, all bivalue with the same pair — swap the pair and the puzzle still works → invalid. Break the deadly pattern.

Deadly form: rectangle, two boxes, four cells all {5,8} only — swapping 5 and 8 gives a second solution.  
Must break it.  
Type 1: one cell has an extra candidate — place that digit or the deadly pattern survives.  
Type 2: outside the rectangle, a line has only one place left for one digit of the pair — force it there.

On the board: r2c3, r2c7, r8c3, r8c7; r2c7 also has 2 — place **2**.

Needs exactly two boxes and four bivalue corners.

Practice Type 1 and Type 2.

Homework or app.

**Mnemonic: Rectangle across two boxes, same deadly pair — unique solution forces a break; extra candidate or external lock wins.**
